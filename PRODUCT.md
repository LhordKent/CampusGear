# CampusGear

## Register
product

## Users and purpose
Borrowers reserve campus equipment. Custodians approve requests and record releases and returns. Administrators manage inventory, accounts, borrower access, and audit history. The approved stack is .NET 8 MVC and SQL Server.

## Design authority
The supplied Figma file and its saved generated markup are the visual authority. Use its components, exported assets, layouts, and states; real database records replace sample names, counts, dates, and statuses.

## Principles
Preserve the designed role-specific workflows and real authorization. Reuse the shared shells and controls. Display actual empty and error states. Support 1440 by 900, 1920 by 1080, tablet, and phone widths with normal vertical scrolling.

## Accessibility and anti-references
Keep keyboard access, visible focus, responsive forms, and locally served fonts and images. Avoid unrequested redesigns, invented icons, fake live records, fixed canvases, and temporary Figma asset URLs.
